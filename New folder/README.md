# StoryBuilder — Instagram Reels & Video Production Landing Page

A sleek, modern dark-themed landing page engineered specifically for **The Story Builder — Reels Production & Video Editing Service**.

## Features & Highlights
- **Interactive 9:16 Video Player**: Embedded YouTube Shorts modal cards with zero clutter, real view counters, and smooth playback.
- **Hero Section**: Value prop, rating badge, trust markers, and call-to-actions.
- **Industry Marquee**: Animated infinite scrolling ticker for Real Estate, E-Commerce, Gyms, Doctors, Tech Founders, Cafes & Agencies.
- **Transparent Packages**:
  - Starter 4 Reels Pack (₹9,999)
  - Growth 8 Reels Pack (₹18,999 - Most Popular)
  - 6 Reels Launch Pack (₹14,999)
  - 15 Reels Content Machine (₹32,999)
  - 30 Reels VIP Agency Machine (₹59,999)
- **Interactive FAQ Accordion**: Instant answers to locations, equipment, turnarounds, and scripts.
- **Booking Enquiry Form**: Direct WhatsApp submission with pre-filled message generator.
- **Responsive & Lightweight**: Pure HTML, CSS, and JS with zero dependencies.

## Structure
- `index.html` — Full page structure, markup, video modal & forms
- `css/style.css` — Modern dark theme design system (`:root`), typography, glassmorphism, responsive breakpoints
- `js/script.js` — Lightbox video player, navbar scroll, pricing tab filters, accordion, stat counters, and WhatsApp form handler
