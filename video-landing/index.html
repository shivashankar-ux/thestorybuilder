Questions before you book

How much does a shoot and edit cost?

It depends on the number of videos, shoot days and location. Packages start at ₹XX,XXX. Send a short brief and we'll reply with a fixed quote, so there are no surprises later.

How long does it take?

Most projects shoot within a week of confirmation. The first cut is ready 5 to 7 working days after the shoot, depending on length and complexity.

Do I need a script or idea ready?

No. Bring your goal and your product or service. We help with concept, script and shot list before the shoot day.

Can you shoot outside Hyderabad?

Yes. We travel across India. Travel and stay costs are quoted upfront.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of changes on every edited video. Send feedback in one list per round so we can turn it around quickly. Extra rounds are quoted separately.

Can you edit footage I've already shot?

Yes. Choose "Editing only" in the quote form and tell us what you have. We'll review it and quote the edit.

Who owns the final videos?

You do. Once the project is paid, the final videos are yours to use on any channel. Raw footage is available on request.
